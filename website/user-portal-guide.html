Customer Portal Guide

Manage ownership, computers, purchases, Maintenance and Support, downloads, account security, and support requests from one verified account.

Updated customer reference

Everything you can manage in the Customer Portal

Use this guide to verify your account, understand license and Maintenance and Support status, purchase or upgrade safely, manage computers, download eligible releases, request support, and protect your account.

01 · Account access

Verify your email, create a password, and sign in

Select the verification link sent to your registered email address and create a Customer Portal password. Verification and password-reset links are single-use and expire automatically. If you forget your password, use Reset your password on the sign-in page. For privacy, the recovery form displays the same confirmation whether or not an account matches the email.

Security reminder The portal automatically signs you out after 10 minutes without activity. Save changes before leaving the portal unattended.
02 · Overview

See your account status at a glance

Overview welcomes you by your full customer or company name and displays your current license as Active, its listener allowance, and the exact Maintenance and Support Until date. Beginning 90 days before coverage expires, a daily reminder shows the expiration date, days remaining, and a renewal button.

When the installed application is behind the current release, Overview shows the installed and latest versions, how many releases behind you are, a release-notes link, and either Download Latest Version or Renew Maintenance and Support.

03 · Licenses

Review ownership without exposing activation keys

Licenses shows the masked key ending, license level, Active status, issue date, listener allowance, and Maintenance and Support expiration. Complete activation keys are never displayed.

  1. Confirm your Customer Portal password; confirmation remains valid for five minutes.
  2. Select Resend Activation Key for the correct license.
  3. The key is sent only to the verified email address on the account.

Resending requires active Maintenance and Support. When coverage has expired, the button is disabled and the portal provides the expiration date plus a direct renewal action.

04 · Plans & maintenance

Purchase, upgrade, evaluate, or renew

Your current license is visually highlighted. The portal compares Lite, Pro, and Enterprise capabilities and offers the correct action: first purchase, upward upgrade, or clearly labeled additional license. A selected license from the public Buy page remains selected after sign-in.

  • Confirm your password before creating a secure checkout.
  • Purchases are one-time payments; paid licenses are permanent.
  • Optional annual Maintenance and Support renewal adds one year of updates and assisted support.
  • Eligible installations can start a server-authorized Five-Day Promotional Trial from the application—no key is required.
05 · Purchase & Billing

Review payments and download receipts

Purchase & Billing separates license purchases, upgrades, and Maintenance and Support renewals. Each row shows payment status, the associated masked license, amount, transaction reference, and a receipt that can be printed or saved as PDF. Payment credentials and complete activation keys are never shown.

06 · Computers

Link computers and move a license securely

In the application, open Settings → License and select Link This Computer. The app displays a single-use code and opens the Customer Portal. Sign in with a verified email, confirm your password, review the requesting Windows computer, select an eligible license, and approve the link. The app checks for approval and activates automatically.

  1. Start the link from the computer you want to activate.
  2. Approve the code within 10 minutes; expired and used codes cannot be reused.
  3. Select a license that is not active on another computer.
  4. Return to the app and wait for the linked-and-activated confirmation.

To transfer a license, confirm your portal password, enter a transfer reason, and deactivate the old computer first. If the old device is lost or unavailable, submit a License Issue in Support for identity-verified recovery. Backup activation keys follow this same portal flow and never establish ownership on their own.

07 · Downloads

Know when an update is available

Downloads compares the latest version reported by your active installation with the current published release. It displays the installed version, latest version, release date, versions behind, and release notes.

Active Maintenance and Support unlocks Download Latest Version. If coverage has expired, the available version remains visible, but the download action changes to Renew Maintenance and Support.

08 · Support

Submit and follow a private support request

Choose Bug Report, Feature Request, License Issue, or Other Issue. Add a clear subject and description, then optionally attach one PNG, JPG, TXT, PDF, or ZIP file up to 2 MB. Do not include activation keys, passwords, payment details, or customer receipt contents.

Your support history shows the private reference, status, conversation, and replies. A request saved locally but awaiting secure submission includes a retry action.

09 · Preferences & security

Control privacy and protect your account

Preferences lets you update the displayed customer or company name, manage optional product-news and privacy-safe analytics consent, and review the recorded consent history.

Two-step verification

  1. Select Start two-step setup.
  2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app or enter the setup key.
  3. Enter the six-digit code and save the one-time recovery codes.

To disable two-step verification, re-enter the account password, provide a valid authenticator or recovery code, type DISABLE, and confirm the warning. Every active portal session is revoked and a security notification is sent.

Privacy actions include downloading your account data and closing portal access after recent password confirmation. Closing portal access does not cancel or delete a permanent software license or required transaction records.

Information the portal deliberately does not show

  • Complete activation keys on screen
  • Receipt text, raw ESC/POS bytes, barcodes, or receipt images
  • Passwords, authenticator secrets, or existing recovery codes
  • Payment credentials or private listener addresses
